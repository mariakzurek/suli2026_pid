# Cooper — Week 4 Decisions

## Momentum cutoff for binary K/π model

Chosen: --p-max 3.2 GeV

Rationale: From the range 0-2.5 GeV, the contribution to kaon contamination from protons is almost zero, from 2.5-3.2 the proton contribution is more pronounced, but still low, past this point proton contributions become non-negligable. Theta is also stable for the most part through the 3-5 theta range allowing it to be pushed a little into the 3-3.5 range 3.2 was chosen as an inbetween.
